## 🚀 How to Get Started (3 Simple Steps)

### **Step 1: Connect Your Data Sources**

The AI needs data to work with. Connect at least one:

**Option A: Bank Integration (Recommended - If Available)**
1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Banking**
2. Click **Connect Bank Account** (via Mono)
3. Authorize TaxMaster to read transactions
4. AI will automatically import and categorize transactions

*Note: Bank integration requires Mono API configuration. If not available, use CSV/Excel import.*

**Option B: Manual Import**
1. Go to **Transactions → Import**
2. Upload CSV/Excel file with your transactions
3. Map columns (Date, Description, Amount, Type)
4. AI will categorize and suggest tax treatment

**Option C: Manual Entry**
1. Navigate to **Transactions → Add New**
2. Enter transaction details
3. AI will suggest category and tax implications in real-time

---

### **Step 2: Review AI Categorizations**

Once data is imported:

1. Go to **Dashboard → AI Insights**
2. Review AI-suggested transaction categories
3. Accept/reject suggestions with one click
4. The AI learns from your corrections and improves over time

**What to look for:**
- Green check = High confidence (90%+)
- Yellow warning = Medium confidence (70-89%) – review recommended
- Red flag = Low confidence or anomaly detected – manual review needed

---

### **Step 3: Generate Your Tax Returns**

When you're ready to file:

1. Navigate to the tax type you want to file:
   - **Taxes → PAYE** (for monthly payroll taxes)
   - **Taxes → VAT** (for monthly value-added tax)
   - **Taxes → WHT** (for withholding tax)
   - **Taxes → CIT** (for annual corporate income tax)

2. Click **Generate Return** or **AI Workflow**

3. Review the AI-generated return:
   - All calculations are shown with breakdowns
   - Supporting schedules are auto-populated
   - Compliance checks run automatically

4. Export your return:
   - **Download PDF** for your records
   - **Export XML/JSON** for FIRS portal submission
   - **Print** for physical filing (if required)

## 💡 Pro Tips from Our Team

**1. Start with last month's data**
Import your previous month's transactions first. Let the AI categorize them, review the results, and correct any errors. This trains the AI to understand your business better.

**2. Use the AI Chat Assistant**
Ask questions in plain English:
- "What's my VAT liability for March?"
- "When's my next PAYE deadline?"
- "How much CIT will I owe this year?"
- "What reliefs am I eligible for?"

**3. Review confidence scores**
Don't blindly accept all AI suggestions. Review items with yellow/red flags. Your feedback makes the AI smarter for next time.

**4. Set up recurring imports**
If you use bank integration, enable automatic daily sync. This keeps your data fresh and taxes calculated in real-time.

**5. Export early, submit on time**
Generate returns a few days before deadlines. This gives you time to review before submission to FIRS/State IRS portals.
