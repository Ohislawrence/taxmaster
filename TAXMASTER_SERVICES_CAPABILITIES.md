## Our Process

### 1. **Seamless Onboarding**
Getting started with TaxMaster is straightforward:
- **Business Registration**: Complete a simple profile setup with your business details, tax identification numbers (TIN), and industry information
- **Integration Setup**: Connect your existing accounting systems or start fresh with our platform
- **Configuration**: Our AI analyzes your business type and automatically configures appropriate tax types, filing schedules, and compliance requirements
- **Team Setup**: Add staff members with role-based access controls to ensure proper oversight

### 2. **Automated Data Collection**
TaxMaster works intelligently in the background:
- **Transaction Monitoring**: Capture income, expenses, and financial transactions automatically
- **Smart Categorization**: Our AI engine categorizes transactions according to Nigerian tax regulations
- **VAT Classification**: Automatically identifies standard-rated (7.5%), zero-rated (0%), and exempt transactions
- **Staff Payroll Integration**: Seamlessly track employee compensation for accurate PAYE calculations
- **Document Management**: Securely store receipts, invoices, and supporting documentation

### 3. **Intelligent Tax Calculation**
Our sophisticated calculation engines ensure accuracy:
- **Real-time Computation**: Taxes are calculated automatically as transactions occur
- **Multi-Tax Support**: Simultaneous handling of PAYE, CIT, VAT, WHT, CGT, and Stamp Duties
- **Progressive Brackets**: Accurate application of Nigerian tax brackets and relief allowances
- **State-Specific Rules**: Compliance with all 36 states plus FCT requirements
- **Relief Optimization**: Automatic calculation of CRA, pension, NHF, NHIS, and other applicable reliefs

### 4. **Proactive Compliance Management**
Stay ahead of deadlines with our compliance system:
- **Deadline Tracking**: Monitor all filing deadlines across multiple tax types
- **Automated Reminders**: Receive notifications 7, 3, and 1 day before due dates via email and SMS
- **Status Dashboard**: Real-time visibility into compliance status with color-coded priority indicators
- **Penalty Prevention**: Automated calculations for penalties and interest on overdue obligations
- **Compliance Calendar**: Interactive calendar view of all upcoming obligations

### 5. **FIRS Integration & Filing** *(Coming Soon)*
Prepare for direct submission to Federal Inland Revenue Service:
- **E-Invoice Generation**: FIRS-compliant electronic invoice creation ready for submission
- **Automated Submission**: Queue-based submission system architecture ready for single or batch invoices
- **Status Tracking**: Built-in tracking system ready for real-time monitoring of submission approval status
- **Reference Management**: Track FIRS reference numbers and submission IDs once API access is active
- **Export for Manual Filing**: Download FIRS-compliant returns for manual submission until direct API is live
- **Error Handling**: Intelligent validation and error checking before submission

### 6. **AI-Powered Assistance**
Expert guidance at every step:
- **Transaction Analysis**: AI reviews transactions for VAT treatment and tax implications
- **Compliance Recommendations**: Intelligent suggestions to optimize tax positions
- **Anomaly Detection**: Identify unusual patterns or potential errors before filing

### 7. **Secure Payment Processing**
Integrated payment workflows:

## Core Capabilities

### Tax Calculation Engines

#### 1. **PAYE (Pay As You Earn) Calculator**
*Comprehensive employee income tax management*

**Key Features:**
- Progressive tax bracket calculation (7% - 24% across 6 brackets)
- Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA) optimization
- Automatic pension contribution relief (8% of basic salary)
- National Housing Fund (NHF) deductions (2.5%)
- National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) processing (5%)
- Life assurance premium relief (up to limits)
- Monthly, quarterly, and annual computation support
- Bulk employee processing
- Remittance report generation

**Output:**
- Individual employee tax breakdown
- Monthly PAYE remittance schedules
- Annual tax certificates
- Compliance reports for Nigeria Tax Authority

---

#### 2. **CIT (Companies Income Tax) Calculator**
*Corporate tax management with minimum tax protection*

**Key Features:**
- 30% flat rate on assessable profit
- Minimum tax calculation (higher of 0.5% turnover, 0.25% gross assets, or 0.25% paid-up capital)
- Advance tax computation for quarterly payments
- Capital allowance tracking
- Loss carry-forward management
- Pioneer status handling
- Investment incentive computation

**Output:**
- Quarterly CIT estimates
- Annual CIT computation
- Minimum tax analysis
- Tax planning recommendations

---

#### 3. **VAT (Value Added Tax) Calculator**
*Comprehensive VAT management at 7.5%*

**Key Features:**
- Input VAT vs Output VAT calculation
- Automatic exempt item identification
- Zero-rated transaction handling
- Claim validation for input VAT
- Partial exemption calculations
- VAT-inclusive and VAT-exclusive support
- Monthly return preparation

**Supported Categories:**
- Standard-rated supplies (7.5%)
- Zero-rated exports and exempt services
- Diplomatic and humanitarian exemptions
- Basic food items and medical services
- Educational materials and baby products

**Output:**
- Monthly VAT 001 returns
- Input/Output VAT reconciliation
- Claimable VAT analysis
- Category-wise VAT breakdown

---

#### 4. **WHT (Withholding Tax) Calculator**
*Transaction-based withholding tax management*

**Key Features:**
- Category-specific rate determination
- Bulk withholding calculation
- Vendor WHT tracking
- Certificate generation
- Quarterly remittance schedules

**Supported Categories:**
- Dividends: 10%
- Interest: 10%
- Rent: 10%
- Royalties: 10%
- Contracts: 5%
- Professional fees: 5%
- Commission: 10%
- Technical services: 10%

**Output:**
- WHT schedules by category
- Vendor statements
- WHT credit certificates
- Quarterly remittance reports

#### 5. **CGT (Capital Gains Tax) Calculator**
*Capital gains computation with exemptions*

**Key Features:**
- 10% flat rate computation
- Disposal value tracking
- Acquisition cost and incidental cost deductions
- Exemption identification (government securities, principal residence, life insurance)
- Multi-asset processing

**Output:**
- Individual asset gain/loss
- Annual CGT summary
- Exemption documentation
- Filing requirements

#### 6. **Stamp Duties Calculator**
