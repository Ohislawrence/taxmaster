## Common Questions

**Q: Will this work with QuickBooks Desktop?**  
Currently supports QuickBooks Online only. Desktop sync coming Q3 2026.

**Q: What if I have data in both QuickBooks and TaxMaster?**  
Duplicates are automatically detected and merged. Your existing TaxMaster transactions stay intact.

**Q: Can I sync historical data?**  
Yes! Import up to 5 years of QuickBooks history (QuickBooks API limit).

**Q: Does this cost extra?**  
QuickBooks integration is included in Basic plan and above. No additional fees.

**Q: What happens if my QuickBooks subscription expires?**  
Your synced data remains in TaxMaster. New syncs pause until you reactivate QuickBooks.

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
Absolutely. We use OAuth 2.0, encrypt all credentials, and never store your QuickBooks password. Your tokens are encrypted at rest and in transit.
